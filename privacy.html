Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

OMV GDrive Backup is a personal, single-user tool created solely to back up data to Google Drive.

Information Collection and Usage

This application accesses Google Drive exclusively to upload, store, and manage backup files generated by the user. No personal data, user content, or information is collected, shared, stored on external third-party servers, or used for any other purpose.
